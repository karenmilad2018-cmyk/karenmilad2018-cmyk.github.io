How I see this work

Funding committees read many applications, and the strong ones answer three questions quickly: what problem, why you, and how the money comes back. I build your file around those answers and around each entity’s own template, whether that is a Monsha’at programme, the Social Development Bank, SIDF, Kafalah or a private fund. The decision stays with them; my job is to make your case clear and complete.
