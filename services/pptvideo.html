How I see this work

A video that will not play in front of an audience is worse than no video. I embed and compress every clip, test playback on another machine, and keep the file small enough to email, so the moment you press play, it simply works.

MP4 is best; MOV and other formats are converted.

Yes. Record it on your phone and I sync it with the slides.
