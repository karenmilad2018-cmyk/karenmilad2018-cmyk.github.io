How I see this work

An exact copy is about details most people don’t notice until they are wrong: a margin two millimetres off, a line slightly thicker, a font that is almost the same. I rebuild the document element by element and compare it with the original side by side, so the copy can stand in for the original.

How exact will it be?

Layout, spacing, colours and tables match the original. If the exact font is not available, I use the closest one and tell you.

Can you recreate an official or company form?

Yes, for your own use or with permission. I don’t recreate official seals or signatures.

Which program will I receive?

Word, Excel or PowerPoint, whichever you need, plus a PDF.
