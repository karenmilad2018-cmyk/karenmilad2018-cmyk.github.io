How I see this work

A good presentation is a story before it is a design. I build the storyline first, so your conclusion comes early and every slide earns its place, then design around it. For a ten-minute talk, that usually means 8–12 slides, not 30.

Steps and milestones

  1. Storyline: Opening, key points and closing agreed with you.
  2. Draft: Content placed slide by slide (written by me in Premium).
  3. Design: Visuals, charts and layout for each slide.
  4. Polish: Timing, speaker notes and final review.
