How I see this work

A good presentation is a story before it is a design. I build the storyline first, so your conclusion comes early and every slide earns its place, then design around it. For a ten-minute talk, that usually means 8–12 slides, not 30.

Choose a package

BasicUp to 10 slides from your text$101–3 business days
StandardUp to 20 slides + speaker notes$181–3 business days
PremiumUp to 30 slides + I write the content$303–5 business days

What you get

  • Storyline: opening, key points, closing
  • Custom slide design for your content
  • Content writing in the Premium package
  • Speaker notes on request
  • Editable PowerPoint + PDF

What I pay attention to

  • A clear start, middle and end
  • One idea per slide, no walls of text
  • Visuals that explain, not decorate
  • Timing: about one minute per slide

Steps and milestones

  1. 1. Storyline
    Opening, key points and closing agreed with you.
  2. 2. Draft
    Content placed slide by slide (written by me in Premium).
  3. 3. Design
    Visuals, charts and layout for each slide.
  4. 4. Polish
    Timing, speaker notes and final review.

Questions about this service
