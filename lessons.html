How I see this work

Students remember what they practise recalling, not what they read twice. So my lesson slides are built around short recall moments, a change of activity every 10–15 minutes, and outcomes written with Bloom’s taxonomy and aligned with how you will assess them.

What I pay attention to

  • Constructive alignment: outcomes, activities and assessment match
  • Universal Design for Learning: clear fonts, captions, colour-blind safe
  • Cognitive load: one concept per slide
  • Your curriculum or framework, such as AQF or national standards

Steps and milestones

  1. Outcomes: Learning outcomes written and aligned with your curriculum.
  2. Lesson flow: Explain, practise, recall and check, in that order.
  3. Slides and activities: Visual slides, quizzes and tasks.
  4. Assessment: Recap quiz and rubric (Standard and Premium).
