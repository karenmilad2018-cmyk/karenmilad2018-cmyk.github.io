+

No. Every file is designed by hand, by me personally, and I guarantee its quality.

How fast can you deliver?

+

Quick jobs are delivered in 1–3 business days and larger ones in 3–5. Big projects are split into milestones, each delivered in 3–5 business days, with a progress update every working day.

Do I have to pay upfront?

+

You get a detailed quotation first. Then you fund a milestone on Freelancer.com: the money stays protected by Freelancer.com and is released to me only after you approve the work.

Can you convert my PDF or Word file to PowerPoint?

+

Yes. PDF, Word, Google Slides, Keynote and scanned images are rebuilt as fully editable PowerPoint slides.

Do you design Arabic presentations?

+

Yes. Arabic with correct right-to-left layout, plus English, German, Spanish and bilingual slides.

Can you write the content too?

+

Yes. Send the topic and I’ll write, simplify and design the slides, with speaker notes if you need them.
