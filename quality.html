FAQ

Common questions

How long does ISO 9001 documentation take?

For a small organisation, usually 2–4 weeks, delivered in three milestones of 3–5 business days each. Timing starts once I receive the information I need from you.

Do you guarantee certification?

No. The certification body decides. I make sure your documents and records meet the standard and run a readiness check before the audit.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work through video calls and a shared folder, so location is not a barrier.

What do you need from us?

An organisation chart, a short description of your processes, any existing documents, and one contact person who can answer questions.

How do we pay?

Per milestone, after you receive each part. Fiverr, Freelancer, PayPal, bank transfer or Western Union.
