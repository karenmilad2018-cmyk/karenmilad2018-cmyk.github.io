My method

Four tools I use in every quality project

Simple tools, used properly. This is how I find the real problem, rate the risk and write controls your team can follow.

1 · Brainstorming

Collect every idea first

You send me your situation by WhatsApp or email. I list every problem, cause and idea, then group them before writing anything.

Late deliveriesRecords not filledNo training planOld proceduresCustomer complaintsUnclear roles
2 · Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram

Find the root cause

Each bone is one family of causes: People, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement and Environment (the 6M). The real cause is fixed once, not the symptom every day.

3 · Risk analysis

Rate every risk: severity × likelihood

Each risk gets a score from 1 to 25. Red risks get a control first. After the control, the risk moves down the matrix, and the register shows it.

How long does ISO 9001:2015 documentation take?

For a small organisation, usually 2–4 weeks, in three milestones of 3–5 business days each, starting once I have your information.

Do you guarantee certification?

No. The certification body decides. I make sure your documents and records meet the standard and give you a readiness check before the audit.

Do you visit us or hold meetings?

No. Everything is done by WhatsApp, email and a shared folder, so location is never a problem.

Do you deliver the training to our workers?

No. I prepare the complete content, and your quality manager delivers it with the trainer guide.

What do you need from us?

An organisation chart, a short description of your processes, any existing documents, and one contact person who answers questions by message.

How do we pay?

Per milestone, after you receive each part: Fiverr, Freelancer, PayPal, bank transfer or Western Union.
