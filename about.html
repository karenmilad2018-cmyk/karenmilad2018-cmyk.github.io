Hi, I’m Karen.

Quality consultant and designer.

I turn complicated work into files people understand and use.

My journey

  1. Studying

    I studied microbiology and chose quality management. Between lectures I was already training in factories.

  2. On the production line

    My first job was quality control in a factory. I learned that a rule only works when the person doing the job understands it.

  3. Building quality systems

    I built quality systems and checked them against ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO/IEC 17025 and HACCP. I learned to turn a thick standard into simple documents.

  4. Training people

    I trained managers and workers. One clear picture explained more than ten pages, so I started designing.

  5. Learning to design and build

    Presentations, Word, Excel, and then code. I built this website myself.

  6. Full-time freelancer

    Today I work with clients around the world in English, Arabic, German and Spanish. I do every file myself.

What I learned, and how I use it in your file

1Understand before you start

I ask the right questions first and send you a detailed quotation PDF with the plan.

2One system, every time

Styles, numbering and templates that keep your file clean when you edit it later.

3Clear beats long

One idea per slide, short headings, tables instead of long paragraphs.

4Check before it leaves

I check every file line by line before you receive it.
